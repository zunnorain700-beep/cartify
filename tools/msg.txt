Match the original recording and never downgrade audio

Matching used to accept anything within 20 seconds and fall back to the
first hit, which saved a Bonus Track edit as the real song and could not
tell an official upload from a lyrics re-upload.

- score candidates on duration, title words, artist words and variant tags
  instead of one length threshold; reject below the bar rather than guess
- widen the search to 10 candidates (still one metadata call)
- prefer official/Topic/VEVO audio and penalise remix, live, acoustic,
  karaoke and lyrics re-uploads
- audio quality V5 to V0 with abr-sorted bestaudio: ~225 kbps, the least
  lossy MP3 this pipeline can produce
- bound the per-query pick cache so it cannot grow for the process lifetime
